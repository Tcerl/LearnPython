## ĐỀ THI SỐ 1 – CHỦ ĐỀ: HOMETOWN

Nguồn kiến thức tham chiếu: Speaking 2 B1 (Topic 1: Hometown) & Complete PET Units 1–2 (Homes and habits / Student days)

### 1. Hướng Dẫn & Bài Nói Mẫu Speaking Part 2

Dàn ý ghi chú 1 phút (Notes):

- Location: in the north/south/centre of Vietnam, coastal city / peaceful countryside, distance from the capital.

- Famous for: historical buildings, traditional food, friendly locals, beautiful scenery (beaches, mountains, rivers).

- Activities: farming, working in offices/factories, going to local markets, relaxing in parks or cafés in the evening.

- Feelings: proud of my hometown, peaceful atmosphere, miss it when studying/working away, always love coming back.

Bài nói mẫu hoàn chỉnh (Sample Answer - Band B1/B2, ~2 phút):

I would like to talk about my hometown, which is a peaceful coastal city located in the central part of Vietnam. It takes about two hours to travel there by train from the nearest international airport. Although it is not as huge as Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City, it is a very lively and welcoming place to live.

My hometown is famous for several things. First of all, it has long, sandy beaches with clear blue water, so a lot of tourists come here during the summer holidays. Secondly, it is well known for its delicious and inexpensive seafood, as well as a few ancient temples that were built hundreds of years ago.

People in my hometown have a quite relaxed lifestyle. In the daytime, many people work in tourism, local businesses, or fishing. Early in the morning and late in the afternoon, you can see lots of residents jogging along the beach, doing exercise in the park, or chatting with their neighbours at street-side cafés.

Personally, I feel very proud of my hometown and I have a strong attachment to it. Even though I am now busy with my studies and work, returning home always makes me feel calm and happy because that is where my family and childhood memories are.

Từ vựng & Cấu trúc trọng tâm:

- coastal city (n): thành phố ven biển

- be famous / well known for (phr): nổi tiếng về điều gì

- ancient temple (n): ngôi đền/chùa cổ kính

- relaxed lifestyle (n): lối sống thư thả, nhẹ nhàng

- have a strong attachment to (phr): gắn bó sâu sắc với

- What hobby: reading books / playing the guitar / photography / swimming / cooking.

- When started: three years ago / when I was in secondary school / during a summer holiday.

- Why enjoy: gives me a sense of achievement, helps me relax and connect with people who share the same interest.

Bài nói mẫu hoàn chỉnh (Sample Answer - Band B1/B2, ~2 phút):

I would like to talk about my favourite free-time hobby, which is taking photographs. I am not a professional photographer, so I usually use my smartphone or an old digital camera that my older brother gave me.

I started practising this hobby about three years ago when my family went on a summer trip to Da Lat. The scenery there was so breathtaking that I wanted to capture every moment, from the morning fog over the pine forests to the colourful flower gardens. Since that trip, I have been taking photos whenever I have spare time at weekends.

This hobby brings me a lot of benefits. Firstly, it encourages me to go outdoors and explore new places around my city instead of sitting in front of a computer screen all day. Secondly, it helps me pay attention to small, beautiful details in everyday life that people often ignore.

The main reason why I enjoy photography so much is that it relieves my stress after busy days at school and work. Whenever I look back at the photos I have taken, they bring back wonderful memories with my family and friends.

I would like to talk about my travelling experiences. So far, I have been to about ten different cities and provinces across Vietnam, mostly with my family and university friends during summer holidays.

Whenever I go on a trip, I love doing a variety of activities. In the morning, I usually go sightseeing and visit famous historical buildings or local museums. In the afternoon and evening, I enjoy walking around night markets, tasting local specialities, taking lots of photos, and buying small souvenirs for my relatives.

I enjoy travelling so much because it allows me to escape from my busy daily routine and recharge my batteries. More importantly, travelling broadens my horizons by helping me learn about different cultures and meet friendly people wherever I go.
