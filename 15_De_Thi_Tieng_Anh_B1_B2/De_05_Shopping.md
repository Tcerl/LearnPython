## C. ĐÁP ÁN CHI TIẾT & BÀI NÓI MẪU – ĐỀ SỐ 5

### 1. Bài nói mẫu Speaking Part 2 (SHOPPING):

I would like to talk about my shopping habits. As a student, I mostly shop for everyday necessities such as food, drinks, stationery, and books. Occasionally, when a new season starts or when there is a special occasion, I also buy new clothes and shoes.

Where I go shopping depends on what I need to buy. For fresh fruit, vegetables, and snacks, I usually go to a large supermarket near my house because the prices are fixed and the quality is reliable. However, when I want to buy books, electronics, or clothes, I prefer shopping online or visiting a modern shopping centre with my friends.

I usually do the grocery shopping about twice a week, normally on Wednesday afternoons and Sunday mornings. As for clothes and other personal items, I only shop for them once or twice a month when there are discounts.

Overall, I feel quite positive about shopping. Walking around a shopping mall with my best friends at the weekend is a great way to relax and catch up, as long as I compare prices carefully and don't spend too much money.

Từ vựng & Cấu trúc ăn điểm:

- shopping habits (n): thói quen mua sắm

- everyday necessities (n): nhu yếu phẩm hằng ngày

- fixed prices (n): giá niêm yết cố định

- do the grocery shopping (phr): đi mua thực phẩm/tạp hóa

- stick to a budget (phr): chi tiêu trong ngân sách
