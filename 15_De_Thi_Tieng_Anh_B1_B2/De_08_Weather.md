> ### Oymyakon: Living in the Northern Pole of Cold
>
> If you think winters in your country are cold, imagine living in Oymyakon! Located in eastern Siberia, Russia, this small village of around 500 people is known as the 'Northern Pole of Cold' – the coldest permanently inhabited place on Earth (featured in Complete PET Unit 4).
>
> In January, the average temperature is around -50°C, and the lowest temperature ever recorded there was an incredible -67.7°C. At these extreme temperatures, boiling water thrown into the air freezes into snow before it hits the ground, and ink inside pens freezes solid. Yet daily life in Oymyakon goes on as normal. The local school only closes when the thermometer drops below -52°C!
>
> Living in such freezing conditions requires special habits. Car owners must keep their vehicles in heated garages at night, and they often leave their engines running all day when they stop outside shops; if they turn the engine off for even half an hour, the oil and petrol may freeze. Mobile phones rarely work outdoors because the batteries die within minutes in the icy air, and people who wear glasses have to be careful that the metal frames do not stick to their skin.
>
> Because the ground is permanently frozen, nothing grows there. Villagers cannot grow fruit or vegetables in gardens. Instead, their diet consists mainly of reindeer meat, horse meat, milk and frozen fish, which give them the energy they need to survive the cold. In addition, most homes are heated by burning wood or coal and still have outdoor toilets because underground water pipes freeze and burst easily.
>
> When going outside, residents say that artificial modern jackets freeze stiff in minutes; only traditional boots and coats made of reindeer fur can keep them truly warm. Surprisingly, the short summer in June and July can be quite warm, with temperatures occasionally reaching 30°C! Despite the harsh climate, most villagers are proud of their tough hometown and say they would never swap its clean air and strong community spirit for life in a crowded city.

## C. ĐÁP ÁN CHI TIẾT & BÀI NÓI MẪU – ĐỀ SỐ 8

### 1. Bài nói mẫu Speaking Part 2 (WEATHER):

I would like to talk about my favourite type of weather, which is cool, dry, and mildly sunny weather with a gentle breeze. During this weather, the temperature is usually between 22 and 25 degrees Centigrade – neither too hot nor freezing cold.

In my country, this pleasant weather usually occurs in autumn, from late September to November, right after the hot, humid summer and before the cold winter arrives. The sky is often clear and blue with white clouds.

Whenever the weather is like this, I try to spend as much time outdoors as possible. At weekends, I love going for a walk or cycling around the lake with my friends, taking photographs of autumn leaves, or sitting outside at a street-side café enjoying a cup of hot coffee.

I really love this kind of weather because it makes me feel comfortable, energetic, and cheerful. Unlike rainy or stormy days that make me feel sleepy and lazy, cool sunny days motivate me to study harder and enjoy life.

Từ vựng & Cấu trúc ăn điểm:

- degrees Centigrade (n): độ C (Complete PET Unit 7, p.62)

- mildly sunny / gentle breeze (phr): nắng nhẹ / gió nhẹ

- neither too hot nor freezing cold (phr): không quá nóng cũng không lạnh cóng

- humid (adj): ẩm ướt

- motivate somebody to do something (v): truyền động lực cho ai làm gì
