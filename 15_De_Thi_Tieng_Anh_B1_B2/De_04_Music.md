> ### The Hong Kong Youth Music Festival
>
> The Hong Kong Youth Music Festival returns to Victoria Park this weekend! Now celebrating its tenth anniversary, this annual weekend event is one of Asia's liveliest music festivals for teenagers and young adults. Over forty bands and solo artists will perform across three stages from 11 am to 10 pm on both Saturday and Sunday.
>
> Unlike many commercial festivals, the Youth Music Festival gives young amateurs a chance to shine. Four local school bands that won last month's talent competition will share the main stage with famous singers from Hong Kong, Japan and the UK. Tickets cost $15 per day if booked in advance on our website, or $20 at the gate on the day. Children under twelve can enter free of charge as long as they are accompanied by a paying adult.
>
> If you want to do more than just listen, head over to the Workshop Tent near the East Gate. Here, visitors can pick up and try everything from electric guitars and drums to traditional Chinese flutes, with friendly music teachers on hand to give advice. There are also one-hour songwriting classes at 2 pm and 4 pm. All workshops and songwriting classes are included in your festival ticket, though spaces are limited so arrive early.
>
> The highlight of the weekend will undoubtedly be DJ Jupiter, who will close the festival at 9 pm on Sunday night with a spectacular light and dance show. To help keep the park clean and green, single-use plastic bottles are not allowed inside the park, but you can bring an empty metal bottle and fill it up at our free drinking water stations.
>
> As there is no visitor parking at Victoria Park, we strongly recommend using public transport. Free shuttle buses will run every 15 minutes between Star Ferry Harbour and the park entrance. In case of heavy rain or storms, outdoor performances will take place at the nearby City Indoor Arena.

> ### Indian Films – Actor Amitabh Bachchan Talks About His Experiences
>
> I have spent over 30 years in the Indian film industry and have worked with almost three generations of directors and actors. There was a time when life in the movies was very different. It was slower and everything seemed simpler. Now, there are so many things to be dealt with: the light needs to be right, the equipment needs to be returned, the actors have to be somewhere else. There also used to be a sense of magic about the movie industry and the stars were special. Now they are just one of a crowd.
>
> However, in my experience, the new generation of film-makers take their work seriously and they are all very confident. Sometimes, when a young director is talking to me about a scene, I can see technical difficulties. For example, in the film Aks we needed wild dogs for a particular scene and I asked Rakesh Mehra how we would do that. He said it was not a problem. He found an address on the internet and we shot the scene in Romania. More often, Rakesh is anxious about getting the actors' dates right or sorting out the financial side.

> Most of today's young directors have trained in the United States. They have learnt how to plan their productions in great detail and they are extremely well prepared. Before filming starts, they have already made decisions about the costumes, make-up, camera angles and so on. For an actor it means there's someone taking care of everything. It makes the filming go smoothly. I have little doubt that the future of our film industry is in very good hands.

## C. ĐÁP ÁN CHI TIẾT & BÀI NÓI MẪU – ĐỀ SỐ 4

### 1. Bài nói mẫu Speaking Part 2 (MUSIC):

I would like to talk about my taste in music. Actually, I listen to different genres depending on my mood, but my favourite kind of music is definitely pop ballads and acoustic music. Sometimes, when I need to study or read a book, I also listen to soft instrumental music without lyrics.

I really like pop ballads and acoustic songs because they have gentle, catchy melodies and very meaningful lyrics about life, youth, and friendship. Listening to a warm acoustic guitar voice makes me feel peaceful immediately.

Every day, I spend about one to two hours listening to music. I usually put on my headphones while travelling on the bus, doing housework, or lying in bed for half an hour before going to sleep at night.

Music plays an extremely important role in my life. Whenever I feel stressed, tired, or disappointed about something, my favourite songs always cheer me up and give me positive energy. For me, life would be very boring and colourless without music.

Từ vựng & Cấu trúc ăn điểm:

- taste in music (n): gu âm nhạc

- catchy melody (n): giai điệu bắt tai

- meaningful lyrics (n): lời bài hát ý nghĩa

- cheer somebody up (phr v): làm ai đó vui lên

- positive energy (n): năng lượng tích cực
