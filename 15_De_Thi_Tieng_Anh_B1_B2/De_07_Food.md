## C. ĐÁP ÁN CHI TIẾT & BÀI NÓI MẪU – ĐỀ SỐ 7

### 1. Bài nói mẫu Speaking Part 2 (FOOD):

I would like to talk about my favourite dish, which is Vietnamese beef noodle soup, widely known around the world as Pho. It is considered the national dish of Vietnam and can be eaten for breakfast, lunch, or dinner.

To make a delicious bowl of Pho at home, you need several fresh ingredients. The most important part is the broth, which is made by simmering beef bones for several hours with roasted ginger, onions, cinnamon, and star anise. Besides that, you need soft flat rice noodles, thinly sliced beef, spring onions, lime, chilli, and lots of fresh herbs.

Because preparing the broth takes a lot of time and patience, I don't cook it every day. I usually make it with my mother once or twice a month on Sunday mornings when the whole family is at home.

Pho is my absolute favourite food because it has a wonderful balance of flavours – sweet, salty, spicy, and sour. More importantly, cooking and enjoying hot bowls of Pho together always brings my family closer.

Từ vựng & Cấu trúc ăn điểm:

- national dish (n): món ăn quốc dân / truyền thống

- fresh ingredients (n): nguyên liệu tươi ngon

- simmer the broth (phr): ninh/hầm nước dùng

- thinly sliced beef (n): thịt bò thái lát mỏng

- balance of flavours (n): sự cân bằng hương vị
