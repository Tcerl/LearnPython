## C. ĐÁP ÁN CHI TIẾT & BÀI NÓI MẪU – ĐỀ SỐ 2

### 1. Bài nói mẫu Speaking Part 2 (HOBBIES):

I would like to talk about my favourite free-time hobby, which is taking photographs. I am not a professional photographer, so I usually use my smartphone or an old digital camera that my older brother gave me.

I started practising this hobby about three years ago when my family went on a summer trip to Da Lat. The scenery there was so breathtaking that I wanted to capture every moment, from the morning fog over the pine forests to the colourful flower gardens. Since that trip, I have been taking photos whenever I have spare time at weekends.

This hobby brings me a lot of benefits. Firstly, it encourages me to go outdoors and explore new places around my city instead of sitting in front of a computer screen all day. Secondly, it helps me pay attention to small, beautiful details in everyday life that people often ignore.

The main reason why I enjoy photography so much is that it relieves my stress after busy days at school and work. Whenever I look back at the photos I have taken, they bring back wonderful memories with my family and friends.

Từ vựng & Cấu trúc ăn điểm:

- spare time / free time (n): thời gian rảnh rỗi

- breathtaking scenery (n): phong cảnh đẹp ngoạn mục

- capture a moment (phr): lưu giữ khoảnh khắc

- relieve stress (phr): giải tỏa căng thẳng

- bring back memories (phr): gợi lại những kỷ niệm
