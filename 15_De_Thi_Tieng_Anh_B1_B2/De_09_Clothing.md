> ### Renting Clothes Instead of Buying Them
>
> Until recently, renting clothes was something people only did for very formal occasions, such as weddings or graduation balls. However, a growing number of young people are now using smartphone apps and websites to rent their everyday fashion instead of buying it.
>
> On these platforms, members can borrow everything from everyday winter coats and designer jeans to handbags and sportswear for a few days or a whole month. Studies show that the average person wears a new piece of clothing only three or four times before leaving it at the back of the wardrobe. Renting allows fashion lovers to enjoy wearing different styles without filling their bedrooms with clothes they hardly ever use.
>
> Twenty-three-year-old university graduate Hannah Brooks first tried a rental app last year when she needed a smart jacket for an important job interview. 'The jacket cost £200 in the shops, which I couldn't afford,' she explains, 'but I rented it for four days for just £20. I got the job, and I felt so confident wearing something high-quality.'
>
> The process is surprisingly simple. Once you choose an item online, it is delivered to your door in a reusable bag. At the end of the rental period, you put the item back into the bag and drop it off at a local post office. You don't even have to wash or iron the clothes before returning them – the rental company handles all the dry-cleaning using eco-friendly methods. The rental fee also includes insurance for minor damage like a lost button or a broken zip.
>
> Environmentalists welcome the trend because the fashion industry produces millions of tonnes of textile waste every year. When fifty people share the same coat over two winters, far fewer raw materials and chemicals are needed. Seeing how popular these apps have become, several famous high-street fashion brands have recently launched their own rental sections. 'I haven't bought a single new item of clothing for eight months,' says Hannah, 'and my friends always compliment me on my outfits!'

### 1. Bài nói mẫu Speaking Part 2 (CLOTHING):

I would like to talk about my favourite item of clothing, which is a classic dark blue denim jacket. It has two small pockets on the chest and silver buttons down the front.

I bought this jacket last November at a popular clothing shop in a shopping centre near my house. I was looking for a warm jacket for the winter, and I was really lucky because it was on sale at half price.

It is a very versatile piece of clothing, so I wear it on many different occasions. I often put it on when I go to university on cool mornings, when I hang out with my friends at cafés in the evening, or whenever I go travelling because it has deep inside pockets where I can keep my phone and wallet safely.
