### 1. Đáp án 15 câu Viết lại câu (Ca 2A)

1. `He hasn't written to me since 2021.` *(write - wrote - written; in 2021 ➔ since 2021)*
2. `I have never visited Ha Long Bay before.` *(This is the first time ➔ have never ... before)*
3. `My old bicycle is not as fast as your new motorbike.` *(slower than ➔ not as fast as, hoặc: Your new motorbike is not as slow as my old bicycle)*
4. `Nam is the tallest student (hoặc: person) in my class.` *(So sánh nhất: the + adj-est)*
5. `A hot bowl of beef Pho was cooked by my mother yesterday.` *(Bị động QKĐ: was + V3/ed)*
6. `My wallet was stolen on the bus last night.` *(steal - stole - stolen; bỏ by someone)*
7. `He is not old enough to drive a car.` *(too young ➔ not old enough)*
8. `It was such an interesting film that I watched it twice.` *(interesting bắt đầu bằng nguyên âm i ➔ dùng an)*
9. `Unless you practice speaking every day, you won't pass the B1 exam.` *(Unless = If ... don't)*
10. `If I were you, I would go to bed early before the test day.` *(Lời khuyên If I were you, I would + V)*
11. `Nam asked me if I played badminton on Saturdays.` *(Lùi thì play ➔ played)*
12. `Because of the heavy traffic, I arrived at work late.` *(the traffic was heavy ➔ the heavy traffic)*
13. `I would rather listen to music than watch TV.` *(would rather + V-nguyên mẫu + than + V-nguyên mẫu)*
14. `I spend two hours traveling to Thai Binh by bus.` *(spend + thời gian + V-ing)*
15. `"How about drinking hot ginger tea at this cafe?" she said.` *(How about + V-ing)*
