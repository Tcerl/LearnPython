# NHÓM 1: BIẾN ĐỔI THÌ (QUÁ KHỨ ĐƠN ⟷ HIỆN TẠI HOÀN THÀNH) — 5 DẠNG

### Dạng 1: Bắt đầu làm gì (`started / began` ➔ `have/has + V3/ed` hoặc `have/has been + V-ing`)
- **Quy tắc thép:** Bỏ `started / began`, lấy động từ chính chia `have/has + V3/ed`. Đổi `... ago` ➔ `for ...`; Đổi `in / when ...` ➔ `since ...`.
  1. I started playing badminton four years ago.
     ➔ **I have played badminton for four years.**
  2. She began working at this company in 2020.
     ➔ **She has worked at this company since 2020.**
  3. We started living in Hanoi when I was 10 years old.
     ➔ **We have lived in Hanoi since I was 10 years old.**

### Dạng 2: Lần cuối cùng làm gì (`last + V2/ed` / `The last time...` ➔ `haven't / hasn't + V3/ed`)
- **Quy tắc thép:** Chuyển từ câu khẳng định ở Quá khứ đơn sang câu **PHỦ ĐỊNH** ở Hiện tại hoàn thành (`haven't / hasn't + V3/ed`). Bỏ chữ `last`.
  4. The last time I saw my best friend was in 2022.
     ➔ **I haven't seen my best friend since 2022.**
  5. I last visited my grandparents three months ago.
     ➔ **I haven't visited my grandparents for three months.**
  6. She last wrote to me when she moved to London.
     ➔ **She hasn't written to me since she moved to London.**

### Dạng 3: Đã bao lâu chưa làm gì (`haven't/hasn't + V3/ed + for [thời gian]` ➔ `It is / has been + [thời gian] + since + S + (last) + V2/ed`)
- **Quy tắc thép:** Sau `since` luôn chia **Quá khứ đơn (`V2/ed`)**.
  7. I haven't eaten Pho bo for two weeks.
     ➔ **It is (hoặc: has been) two weeks since I last ate Pho bo.**
  8. It is five years since we last met each other.
     ➔ **We haven't met each other for five years.**

### Dạng 4: Lần đầu tiên làm gì (`This is the first time...` ➔ `never ... before`)
- **Quy tắc thép:** `This is the first time + S + have/has + V3/ed` ⟷ `S + have/has + never + V3/ed + before`.
  9. This is the first time I have visited Ha Long Bay.
     ➔ **I have never visited Ha Long Bay before.**
  10. She has never tried kayaking before.
      ➔ **This is the first time she has tried kayaking.**

### Dạng 5: Hỏi bao lâu (`When did you start...?` ➔ `How long have you...?`)
  11. When did you start learning English?
      ➔ **How long have you learned (hoặc: been learning) English?**
  12. How long have you bought this blue denim jacket? / When did you buy this jacket?
      ➔ **How long have you had this blue denim jacket?** *(Lưu ý: `buy` là hành động ngắn, chuyển sang HTHT đổi thành `had` = sở hữu)*.

# NHÓM 2: CÂU SO SÁNH (COMPARISONS) — 4 DẠNG

### Dạng 6: So sánh hơn ⟷ So sánh không bằng (`more / -er than` ⟷ `not as + adj/adv + as`)
- **Quy tắc thép:** Đảo vị trí 2 chủ ngữ A và B. Giữa `as ... as` bắt buộc là **tính từ/trạng từ NGUYÊN MẪU** (bỏ `-er`, bỏ `more`).
  13. Traveling by plane is faster than traveling by train.
      ➔ **Traveling by train is not as fast as traveling by plane.**
  14. Living in the city is more expensive than living in the countryside.
      ➔ **Living in the countryside is not as expensive as living in the city.**
  15. Nam plays badminton better than me. (`better` là so sánh hơn của `well/good`)
      ➔ **I don't play badminton as well as Nam.**

### Dạng 7: So sánh hơn với từ trái nghĩa (`A > B` ⟷ `B < A` dùng tính từ ngược nghĩa)
  16. My old bicycle is slower than your new motorbike.
      ➔ **Your new motorbike is faster than my old bicycle.**
  17. This test was easier than the previous one.
      ➔ **The previous test was more difficult than this one.**

### Dạng 8: Không ai hơn A ⟷ A là nhất (`No one / Nothing ... as/than` ⟷ `The + -est / most`)
  18. No one in my class is taller than Nam.
      ➔ **Nam is the tallest student in my class.**
  19. No river in the world is longer than the Nile.
      ➔ **The Nile is the longest river in the world.**
  20. I have never read a more interesting book than this one.
      ➔ **This is the most interesting book I have ever read.**

### Dạng 9: Giống nhau / Khác nhau (`the same as` ⟷ `different from` / `as ... as`)
  21. My hobby is the same as yours.
      ➔ **My hobby is not different from yours.**
  22. Tom is the same age as Mary.
      ➔ **Tom is as old as Mary.**

# NHÓM 3: CÂU BỊ ĐỘNG (PASSIVE VOICE) — 4 DẠNG

> **Công thức tổng quát:** Đưa Tân ngữ (O) lên làm Chủ ngữ mới + **`be + V3/ed`** + `(by + Chủ ngữ cũ)`.

### Dạng 10: Bị động Hiện tại đơn (`am / is / are + V3/ed`)
  23. Millions of people in Vietnam drink green tea every day.
      ➔ **Green tea is drunk by millions of people in Vietnam every day.**
  24. They clean the classroom every morning.
      ➔ **The classroom is cleaned every morning.**

### Dạng 11: Bị động Quá khứ đơn (`was / were + V3/ed`) — *HAY RA THI NHẤT!*
  25. My mother cooked a hot bowl of beef Pho yesterday.
      ➔ **A hot bowl of beef Pho was cooked by my mother yesterday.**
  26. Someone stole my bicycle outside the supermarket last night.
      ➔ **My bicycle was stolen outside the supermarket last night.** *(Bỏ `by someone`)*.
  27. My parents built our house ten years ago.
      ➔ **Our house was built by my parents ten years ago.**

### Dạng 12: Bị động Hiện tại hoàn thành (`have / has + BEEN + V3/ed`)
  28. They have canceled the flight because of the storm.
      ➔ **The flight has been canceled because of the storm.**
  29. Someone has just broken the window.
      ➔ **The window has just been broken.**

### Dạng 13: Bị động với Động từ khuyết thiếu (`will / can / must / should + BE + V3/ed`)
  30. You must finish this report before 5 pm.
      ➔ **This report must be finished before 5 pm.**
  31. We can buy tickets online on Shopee.
      ➔ **Tickets can be bought online on Shopee.**

# NHÓM 4: QUÁ... ĐẾN NỖI / ĐỦ... ĐỂ (`TOO / ENOUGH / SO / SUCH`) — 3 DẠNG

### Dạng 14: `so + adj + that + S + can't/couldn't + V` ⟷ `too + adj + (for O) + to V`
- **Quy tắc thép:** Đã dùng `too` thì đi với `to V` (không có `that`, và **bỏ tân ngữ `it/them`** ở cuối câu!).
  32. The coffee was so hot that he couldn't drink it.
      ➔ **The coffee was too hot for him to drink.**
  33. The jacket was so expensive that I couldn't buy it.
      ➔ **The jacket was too expensive for me to buy.**

### Dạng 15: `too + adj + to V` ⟷ `not + [Tính từ trái nghĩa] + enough + (for O) + to V`
- **Quy tắc thép:** `enough` luôn đứng **SAU tính từ** (`old enough`, `tall enough`, `cheap enough`).
  34. He is too young to drive a car.
      ➔ **He is not old enough to drive a car.**
  35. The box is too heavy for her to carry.
      ➔ **She is not strong enough to carry the box.**
  36. The shirt was too expensive for me to buy.
      ➔ **The shirt was not cheap enough for me to buy.**

### Dạng 16: `so + adj + that` ⟷ `such + (a/an) + adj + Danh từ + that`
- **Quy tắc thép:** `so` đi với Tính từ đơn lẻ; `such` đi với một Cụm danh từ (`such a nice day`, `such bad weather`, `such an interesting film`).
  37. The weather was so bad that we stayed at home. *(weather là danh từ không đếm được ➔ không dùng a/an)*
      ➔ **It was such bad weather that we stayed at home.**
  38. The film was so boring that I fell asleep. *(film đếm được số ít ➔ dùng a)*
      ➔ **It was such a boring film that I fell asleep.**

# NHÓM 5: CÂU ĐIỀU KIỆN & LỜI KHUYÊN (`IF / UNLESS / SHOULD`) — 3 DẠNG

### Dạng 17: `If ... not` (Nếu không) ⟷ `Unless` (Trừ khi)
- **Quy tắc thép:** `Unless = If ... not`. Khi thay `If` bằng `Unless`, phải bỏ `don't / doesn't / didn't` và chia lại động từ ở dạng khẳng định! Vế sau giữ nguyên.
  39. If you don't hurry, you will miss the bus.
      ➔ **Unless you hurry, you will miss the bus.**
  40. If she doesn't study harder, she will fail the test. *(Chú ý: bỏ `doesn't` thì `study` phải đổi thành `studies`!)*
      ➔ **Unless she studies harder, she will fail the test.**

### Dạng 18: Đưa lời khuyên (`should / advise` ⟷ `If I were you, I would + V`)
  41. You should review your lessons carefully before the exam.
      ➔ **If I were you, I would review my lessons carefully before the exam.**
  42. The doctor advised him to do more exercise.
      ➔ **"If I were you, I would do more exercise," the doctor said.**

### Dạng 19: Tình huống hiện tại ➔ Câu điều kiện loại 2 (`If + S + V2/ed (were), S + would/could + V`)
- **Quy tắc thép:** Trái với hiện tại (Sự thật là Khẳng định ➔ `If` chia Phủ định quá khứ; Sự thật là Phủ định ➔ `If` chia Khẳng định quá khứ).
  43. I don't have enough money, so I can't buy that laptop.
      ➔ **If I had enough money, I could (hoặc: would) buy that laptop.**
  44. Because the weather is bad today, we can't go camping.
      ➔ **If the weather weren't (hoặc: wasn't) bad today, we could go camping.**

# NHÓM 6: CÂU GIÁN TIẾP / TƯỜNG THUẬT (REPORTED SPEECH) — 4 DẠNG

> **Quy tắc lùi thì & đổi từ:** `am/is ➔ was`, `are ➔ were`, `will ➔ would`, `can ➔ could`, `V1/V2 ➔ V2/had V3`, `today ➔ that day`, `tomorrow ➔ the next day / the following day`, `yesterday ➔ the day before`, `now ➔ then`, `here ➔ there`.

### Dạng 20: Tường thuật câu kể (`said / told sb (that) + S + V-lùi thì`)
  45. "I really love listening to music," Nam said.
      ➔ **Nam said (that) he really loved listening to music.**
  46. "I will visit Ha Long Bay tomorrow," she told me.
      ➔ **She told me (that) she would visit Ha Long Bay the following day.**

### Dạng 21: Tường thuật câu hỏi Yes/No (`asked sb + IF / WHETHER + S + V-lùi thì`)
- **Quy tắc thép:** Phải có `if` hoặc `whether`, và đưa chủ ngữ lên trước động từ (bỏ `do/does/did`, không đảo ngữ!).
  47. "Do you play badminton on Saturdays?" he asked me.
      ➔ **He asked me if (hoặc: whether) I played badminton on Saturdays.**
  48. "Are you ready for the exam?" the teacher asked us.
      ➔ **The teacher asked us if we were ready for the exam.**

### Dạng 22: Tường thuật câu hỏi có từ để hỏi Wh- (`asked sb + Wh-word + S + V-lùi thì`)
  49. "Where do you live?" she asked me.
      ➔ **She asked me where I lived.** *(Cấm viết: where did I live!)*.
  50. "What time does the train leave?" Tom asked.
      ➔ **Tom asked what time the train left.**

### Dạng 23: Tường thuật câu mệnh lệnh, yêu cầu, lời khuyên (`told / asked / advised + O + (not) + TO V`)
  51. "Please turn off your mobile phones," the examiner said to us.
      ➔ **The examiner told (hoặc: asked) us to turn off our mobile phones.**
  52. "Don't stay up too late," my mother said to me.
      ➔ **My mother told me not to stay up too late.**

# NHÓM 7: LIÊN TỪ TƯƠNG PHẢN & NGUYÊN NHÂN (`ALTHOUGH / BECAUSE`) — 2 DẠNG

### Dạng 24: Mặc dù (`Although / Even though + S + V` ⟷ `In spite of / Despite + Cụm danh từ / V-ing`)
- **3 cách biến mệnh đề (`S + V`) thành Cụm danh từ / V-ing:**
  - *Cách 1 (`The + N + be + adj` ➔ `the + adj + N`):* `the weather was bad` ➔ `the bad weather` / `the traffic was heavy` ➔ `the heavy traffic`.
  - *Cách 2 (Cùng chủ ngữ ➔ Dùng `V-ing`):* `Although he was tired, he...` ➔ `In spite of being tired, he...`
  - *Cách 3 (`S + be + adj` ➔ `Sở hữu + Danh từ`):* `Although he was sick/ill` ➔ `In spite of his sickness/illness`.
  53. Although the rain was heavy, they continued playing football.
      ➔ **In spite of the heavy rain, they continued playing football.**
  54. Even though she studied hard, she didn't get a high score.
      ➔ **Despite studying hard, she didn't get a high score.**

### Dạng 25: Bởi vì (`Because / Since / As + S + V` ⟷ `Because of / Due to + Cụm danh từ / V-ing`)
  55. Because the traffic was heavy, I arrived at school late.
      ➔ **Because of the heavy traffic, I arrived at school late.**
  56. He couldn't go to work because he was ill.
      ➔ **Because of his illness (hoặc: being ill), he couldn't go to work.**

# NHÓM 8: 5 CẤU TRÚC ĐẶC BIỆT B1 HAY RA NHẤT (SỞ THÍCH, THỜI GIAN, GỢI Ý, THÓI QUEN, QUY ĐỊNH)

### Dạng 26: Thích cái gì hơn (`like ... better than` ⟷ `prefer + V-ing + TO + V-ing` ⟷ `would rather + V-bare + THAN + V-bare`)
- **Quy tắc thép:**
  - `prefer` đi với **`V-ing ... TO ... V-ing`**.
  - `would rather` đi với **`V-nguyên mẫu ... THAN ... V-nguyên mẫu`**.
  57. I like reading books better than watching TV.
      ➔ **I prefer reading books to watching TV.**
      ➔ **I would rather read books than watch TV.**
  58. She prefers shopping online to going to traditional stores.
      ➔ **She would rather shop online than go to traditional stores.**

### Dạng 27: Tốn bao nhiêu thời gian làm gì (`It takes/took + O + [thời gian] + to V` ⟷ `S + spend/spent + [thời gian] + V-ing`)
- **Quy tắc thép:** `take` đi với **`to V`**; `spend` đi với **`V-ing`**.
  59. It takes me two hours to travel to my hometown by bus.
      ➔ **I spend two hours traveling to my hometown by bus.**
  60. She spent three days finishing her history project.
      ➔ **It took her three days to finish her history project.**

### Dạng 28: Câu gợi ý, rủ rê (`Why don't we / Let's + V-bare` ⟷ `How about / What about / suggest + V-ing`)
- **Quy tắc thép:**
  - `Let's` / `Why don't we` / `Shall we` + **V-nguyên mẫu**.
  - `How about` / `What about` / `I suggest` + **V-ing**.
  61. "Why don't we go to the park this Sunday?" Nam said.
      ➔ **"How about going to the park this Sunday?" Nam said.**
      ➔ **Nam suggested going to the park that Sunday.**
  62. Let's drink hot ginger tea at this roadside cafe.
      ➔ **What about drinking hot ginger tea at this roadside cafe?**

### Dạng 29: Thói quen trong quá khứ (`used to + V-bare` ⟷ `no longer + V` / `not ... anymore`)
- **Quy tắc thép:**
  - `used to + V-bare`: Đã từng làm gì trong quá khứ (bây giờ không còn nữa).
  - Phân biệt với: `be / get used to + V-ing` = Quen với việc gì ở hiện tại!
  63. My father used to smoke a lot, but now he has stopped.
      ➔ **My father no longer smokes.** *(hoặc: My father doesn't smoke anymore).*
  64. Often riding a bicycle in the early morning is not difficult for me because I do it every day.
      ➔ **I am used to riding a bicycle in the early morning.**

### Dạng 30: Quy định, Sự cần thiết, Sở hữu & Các cụm tương đương (`Modal Verbs & Equivalents`)
  65. **It is necessary for you to + V** ⟷ **You must / have to / need to + V**
      - *Ví dụ:* It is necessary for you to bring your ID card to the exam room.
      - ➔ **You must (hoặc: have to) bring your ID card to the exam room.**
  66. **It is not necessary for you to + V** ⟷ **You don't have to / don't need to / needn't + V**
      - *Ví dụ:* It isn't necessary for you to pay for the ticket in advance.
      - ➔ **You don't have to (hoặc: don't need to) pay for the ticket in advance.**
  67. **You are not allowed to + V** / **It is forbidden to + V** ⟷ **You mustn't + V**
      - *Ví dụ:* Visitors are not allowed to take photos inside the museum.
      - ➔ **Visitors mustn't take photos inside the museum.**
  68. **S + belong(s) to + O** ⟷ **S + be + Tính từ sở hữu / Sở hữu cách (`'s`)**
      - *Ví dụ:* That blue denim jacket belongs to my brother.
      - ➔ **That is my brother's blue denim jacket.**
  69. **There is / are ...** ⟷ **S + have / has ...**
      - *Ví dụ:* There are five levels in Charlie's windmill house.
      - ➔ **Charlie's windmill house has five levels.**
  70. **S + be + interested in / keen on / fond of + V-ing** ⟷ **S + enjoy / like / love + V-ing**
      - *Ví dụ:* I really enjoy listening to Den Vau's songs.
      - ➔ **I am very interested in (hoặc: keen on / fond of) listening to Den Vau's songs.**
  71. **S + find + it + adj + to V** ⟷ **It + be + adj + (for O) + to V**
      - *Ví dụ:* I find it easy to play badminton with my friends.
      - ➔ **It is easy for me to play badminton with my friends.**
  72. **S + succeed in + V-ing** ⟷ **S + manage to + V-bare** (Xoay xở làm thành công việc gì)
      - *Ví dụ:* I managed to buy the headphones for half the original price.
      - ➔ **I succeeded in buying the headphones for half the original price.**
  73. **S + lend + O1 + O2** (Cho mượn) ⟷ **S + borrow + O2 + FROM + O1** (Mượn từ ai)
      - *Ví dụ:* Can you lend me your brother's scooter?
      - ➔ **Can I borrow your brother's scooter from you?**
  74. **It is a good idea to + V** ⟷ **You should / ought to + V**
      - *Ví dụ:* It is a good idea to arrive at the interview 15 minutes early.
      - ➔ **You should arrive at the interview 15 minutes early.**
  75. **What is the price of ...? / How much does ... cost?** ⟷ **How much is ...?**
      - *Ví dụ:* How much did that pair of wireless headphones cost?
      - ➔ **What was the price of that pair of wireless headphones?**
