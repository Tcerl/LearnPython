# CẨM NANG VIẾT THƯ & EMAIL B1 (INFORMAL LETTER / EMAIL WRITING)
### Hướng dẫn chinh phục trọn vẹn điểm viết thư trình độ B1

> **MỤC TIÊU BÀI THI VIẾT THƯ B1**:
> - Độ dài: Khoảng **100 từ** (dao động an toàn: 90 - 120 từ).
> - Định dạng: Thư thân mật gửi cho một người bạn hoặc người quen (Informal letter/email).
> - Yêu cầu: Trả lời **đủ tất cả các gợi ý** trong đề bài (thường có 3-4 câu hỏi/gợi ý).
> - Sử dụng từ nối tự nhiên, thì động từ chính xác và câu mở/kết thư lịch sự.

---

## I. CẤU TRÚC 3 PHẦN "BẤT BẠI" CỦA BỨC THƯ B1

```text
1. SALUTATION (Chào hỏi):
   Dear + [Tên bạn], / Hi + [Tên bạn],

2. OPENING (Mở bài - 1-2 câu):
   Cảm ơn vì đã viết thư / Bày tỏ niềm vui khi nhận được thư.

3. BODY (Thân bài - 2-3 đoạn ngắn):
   Trả lời lần lượt 3 gợi ý của đề bài (Dùng các từ nối: First, Also, Besides, Because...).

4. CLOSING (Kết bài - 1-2 câu):
   Lời nhắn gửi phản hồi, hỏi thăm sức khỏe gia đình bạn.

5. SIGN-OFF (Lời chào tạm biệt & Ký tên):
   Best wishes, / Warm regards, / Love,
   [Tên bạn]
```

## II. BỘ "CÂU THẦN CHÚ" MỞ ĐẦU & KẾT THÚC ĂN ĐIỂM NGAY

### 1. Câu mở đầu (Chọn 1 câu học thuộc lòng):
- *Mẫu 1 (Thông dụng nhất):*
  > **Thanks a lot for your lovely email. It was great to hear from you again!**
  *(Cảm ơn nhiều vì bức email dễ thương của bạn. Thật tuyệt khi lại nghe tin từ bạn!)*
- *Mẫu 2 (Bày tỏ niềm vui):*
  > **I'm so glad to receive your letter. I hope you and your family are doing well.**
  *(Mình rất vui khi nhận được thư của bạn. Mình hy vọng bạn và gia đình đều khỏe mạnh.)*
- *Mẫu 3 (Nếu lâu chưa viết):*
  > **Sorry for not writing sooner, but I've been extremely busy with my exams recently.**
  *(Xin lỗi vì đã không viết thư sớm hơn, nhưng gần đây mình cực kỳ bận rộn với các kỳ thi.)*

### 2. Từ nối liên kết thân bài (Linking Words):
- Bắt đầu ý 1: `First of all, ...` / `To begin with, ...`
- Thêm ý 2: `Also, ...` / `Besides, ...` / `In addition, ...`
- Thêm ý 3: `Furthermore, ...` / `Another thing is that ...`
- Đưa ra lý do: `Because ...` / `Since ...`
- Kết quả: `So, ...` / `Therefore, ...`
- Tương phản: `However, ...` / `Although ...`

### 3. Câu kết thúc thư (Chọn 1 câu học thuộc lòng):
- *Mẫu 1:*
  > **Anyway, I have to go now and finish my homework. Write back to me soon!**
  *(Dù sao thì bây giờ mình phải đi làm xong bài tập về nhà rồi. Nhớ viết thư lại cho mình sớm nhé!)*
- *Mẫu 2:*
  > **That's all for now. Please give my best regards to your parents. Hope to see you soon!**
  *(Đó là tất cả tin tức hiện tại. Cho mình gửi lời hỏi thăm tới bố mẹ bạn nhé. Hy vọng sớm gặp lại bạn!)*

## III. 5 BÀI MẪU VIẾT THƯ B1 HOÀN CHỈNH "TRÚNG TỦ"

### Đề 1: Kể về kỳ nghỉ vừa qua (A recent holiday / trip)
**Đề bài:** Your English friend, Alex, asked you about your recent holiday. Write an email to Alex telling him where you went, what you did, and whether you enjoyed it.

**Bài viết mẫu (105 từ):**
> Dear Alex,
>
> Thanks a lot for your email. It was really wonderful to hear from you again!
>
> Last week, I went to Da Nang city with my family for a three-day summer holiday. The weather was fantastic, with bright sunshine and cool sea breezes. 
>
> During the trip, we spent most of our time swimming at My Khe beach and tasting delicious seafood like grilled prawns and crab soup. We also visited the famous Golden Bridge in Ba Na Hills and took lots of stunning photos. 
>
> I enjoyed the trip very much because it helped me relax and spend quality time with my parents.
>
> Write back to me soon!
>
> Best wishes,  
> Nam

---

### Đề 2: Giới thiệu quê hương / Thành phố của bạn (Your hometown)
**Đề bài:** Your friend, Sam, is planning to visit your hometown next month. Write an email to Sam telling him about your hometown, what the best season to visit is, and what you will do together.

**Bài viết mẫu (110 từ):**
> Dear Sam,
>
> I'm so excited to hear that you are coming to visit my hometown next month!
>
> My hometown is Hanoi, the charming capital city of Vietnam. It is well-known for its ancient temples, beautiful lakes, and amazing street food.
>
> In my opinion, autumn is the perfect season to visit because the weather is mild, cool, and dry, which is ideal for walking around. When you arrive, I will take you to explore the lively Old Quarter, try hot Pho noodles, and drink traditional egg coffee by Hoan Kiem Lake.
>
> Let me know your exact arrival date so I can pick you up at the airport.
>
> Warm regards,  
> Linh

---

### Đề 3: Lời mời dự tiệc sinh nhật (Birthday party invitation)
**Đề bài:** You are having a birthday party next weekend. Write an email to your English friend, David, inviting him to the party, giving details of time and place, and suggesting what to bring.

**Bài viết mẫu (100 từ):**
> Dear David,
>
> I hope you are doing well. As you know, my eighteenth birthday is coming next Saturday, and I am organizing a small party to celebrate it.
>
> The party will take place at my house from 6:30 pm to 9:30 pm on November 28th. Most of our classmates will be there. We are going to have a delicious barbecue dinner, play fun board games, and listen to music.
>
> You don't need to bring any expensive gifts, but if you have a guitar, please bring it along so we can sing together.
>
> Please let me know if you can come by Wednesday.
>
> Love,  
> Mai

---

### Đề 4: Chia sẻ về một sở thích mới (A new hobby)
**Đề bài:** You have recently started a new hobby. Write an email to your friend, Emma, describing your new hobby, why you started it, and how you feel about it.

**Bài viết mẫu (108 từ):**
> Dear Emma,
>
> How have you been? I'm writing to share some exciting news with you about my new hobby.
>
> About two weeks ago, I decided to take up swimming. I started it because I often felt tired after sitting in front of the computer for long hours, and my doctor advised me to be more active.
>
> I now go to the local swimming pool three times a week with my brother. Swimming is not only great fun but also helps me stay fit and sleep much better at night. I feel much more energetic and relaxed now.
>
> What hobbies are you doing these days? Tell me in your next letter!
>
> Best wishes,  
> Huy

---

### Đề 5: Lời khuyên học tiếng Anh (Advice on learning English)
**Đề bài:** Your friend, Jack, wants some advice on how to improve his English speaking skills. Write an email to Jack giving him tips and suggesting how you can practice together.

**Bài viết mẫu (106 từ):**
> Dear Jack,
>
> It was great to get your email yesterday. Don't worry about your English speaking skills; everyone finds it challenging at first!
>
> Here are a few tips that really helped me. First, try to listen to English songs or watch short videos every day to copy natural pronunciation. Second, practice speaking English in front of a mirror for 10 minutes each morning to build your confidence.
>
> Also, why don't we practice speaking English together on video calls for 30 minutes every weekend? We can discuss topics like sports, travel, and music.
>
> Let me know what you think!
>
> Your friend,  
> Phong
